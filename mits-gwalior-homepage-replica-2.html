About the Institute

Madhav Institute of Technology & Science, Gwalior was established in 1957. The institute has a long tradition of technical education and aims to create engineers and technocrats capable of contributing to society.

Vision: To create world-class quality engineers and technocrats capable of providing leadership in all spheres of life and society.
